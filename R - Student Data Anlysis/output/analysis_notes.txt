The workbook contains 64000 student-term rows across 24781 masked students.
Only STUDENT_ACADEMY_FLAG has material missingness: 99.59% missing, and every observed value is 1.
Retention is moderately imbalanced but not extreme: 59.7% of rows are marked retained.
Business and Management is the most common study area overall, but Nursing is the largest category within the female cohort while Business and Management leads within the male cohort.
Students marked retained have a much higher average TERM_GPA (3.58) than those not retained (1.4).
The logistic model improves on the naive majority-class baseline: accuracy 0.764 versus baseline 0.76.
All 2025 rows are coded as not retained, so 2025 was excluded from the logistic model as a timing issue rather than a plausible behavioural signal.
